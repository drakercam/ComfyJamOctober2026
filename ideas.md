# Comfy Jam 2026

> Theme is Autumn
> Limitation is "Things Change When you look away"

# Ideas

-- Aesthetics

> 2D top-down
> Pokemon-styled
> Duck Hunt Styled Graphics --- !!!

-- Core Mechanics

> Cone of vision
> Things only move when you're not looking at them
	- Weeping Angels

> Escaping a maze with horror creatures
	- You look away from your direction, maze shifts
	- Cone of vision

> On a horseback-carriage going through the autumn woods
	- Main mechanic looking around and shining flashlight
	- Duck hunt, shooting them with a nerf gun
	- Carrying two things, flashlight and toy gun
	- Flashlight main purpose:
		- immobilize some period of time
	- Toy gun main purpose:
		- shoot the monster in the dark to elimate them
		- round based
	- Flashlight can see everything in the cone its defined,
	  so if a monster gets spotted, they're immobilized briefly so
	  the player can then shoot their last known location. However, the
	  player cannot see the actual monster when shooting, he can only
	  think back to its last location or sometimes you'll see the grass
	  rustle.
	- Main purpose, survive all the rounds. 

> Core components of the game
- Player
	- Two interactables
		- flashlight
		- toy gun
	- Aim via mouse movement
	- Stun enemies
	- Shoot enemies
	- Has lives and can lose them
- Enemies
	- Move, random direction (static, random number between 1-3, move
	  diagonal left/right or move forward)
	- Become immobilized when spotted
	- If they get within a certain distance of cart, player loses life
- Environment
	- Define the area enemies move in
	- Is the game scene
